**Deferred (4), with reasons:**
- `MATH:diagnostic:MATH-APP-009:1`: Deferred. The proposed clause ('both continue at these constant speeds until Deon catches Maya') changes the stem of a fixed form that students have already taken; the uniform-motion reading is the standard one and the 12-mile key is unaffected. Recommend adding the clause in the next diagnostic form edition.
- `MATH:diagnostic:MATH-DAT-DX10:1`: Deferred. The stem already says the X-mark counts are shown in the table, so stimulus and payload agree and the 18 observations / answer 5 are determinate. Testing line-plot reading needs a rendered line-plot visual with an accessible alternative (renderer work), and would change the stimulus of a taken fixed form.
- `PROMPT:review-status-defaults`: Product decision, not changed: tracker-only and original diagnostic records have no reviewStatus, and questionReviewStatus() reads that as final. That is not human approval. Switching the default would put a Provisional label on the live diagnostic and legacy items, so it is isolated and reported for Jed/John to decide. No item was promoted; every revision is provisional; test contentRevisions.test.ts 'review status' documents both facts.
- `MATH:bank:MATH-NUM-006:1`: Optional editorial finding, deferred. Stating that each difference is 2 greater than the one before would hand students the rule the item exists to test (find the growing difference) and would make distractors B (repeat the difference 10) and C (repeat the difference 12) misreadings of the stem rather than pattern errors. The intended continuation 43, 57, 73 is the conventional 'if the pattern continues' reading. Leave to the editorial reviewer to decide between strict determinacy and the pattern-finding demand.

What the suite covers:
- **Answer integrity** (`contentIntegrity.test.ts`): every current question in both tracks through the real scorer. MC has exactly one key and no duplicate choices; every wrong choice scores wrong. MS has the exact count, and swap and too-few answers fail. IC has unique valid mappings, and a wrong option fails. DND zone and pool references are valid. EE accepts every listed form and rejects a wrong value. Passages resolve. Every served item has explanation content. The APP-001 nested `wp` feedback, the DAT-DX09 EE+table, and the diagnostic per-choice fallback are checked.
- **Revision safety** (`contentRevisions.test.ts`): each revision targets an existing ID in its own track, is exactly one version higher, cites real findings, is never `final`, and preserves its original. Feedback-only revisions keep the task and key. Changed content never reuses an ID. Pinned version separation: ALG-004 25/20, APP-011 64/60, GEO-010 189/126, diagnostic REB-004 "wakes". Diagnostic IDs are never added or removed. No provisional item is promoted.
- **Scope** (`scopeHolds.test.ts`): no held version in the full catalog, for any role, or in the diagnostic. The archive keeps all 19 originals. The diagnostic is 50 + 50 in order, or reports blocked slots.
- **Coverage** (`auditCoverage.test.ts`): all 442 audited versions (402 IDs) still exist, and the source has no unaudited IDs. Every finding has exactly one ledger entry, and every `fixed` entry names a revision that cites it. No served item depends on a "Part A". ELA-RC-136 stands alone. DAT-003 and DAT-107 are no longer near-duplicates.
- **Rendering** (`responseFields.test.tsx`, jsdom): DAT-115 and DAT-DX09 show the table and an input. Every multi-select states its count and shows its checkboxes. Inline-choice items render one control per slot.

Not run: a real browser at phone and desktop widths, screen-reader and keyboard passes, the deployed site, Firebase data, or psychometrics. Local tests are not production verification.
