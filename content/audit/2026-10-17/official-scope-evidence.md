# Official SHSAT scope evidence for the Grade 8 product

Checked 2026-10-17. The relevant exam is fall 2026 for 2027 admission. The older “2026 Admissions” guide concerns the previous admissions cycle. Evidence reviewed: current DOE 2027 guide, four current Grade 8 official practice forms A–D (200 ELA and 200 mathematics items), the two Grade 9 mini-forms (26 mathematics items), current DOE test page, Pearson/DOE TEI examples, and NYSED primary standards excerpts. Practice PDFs were read through extracted text/OCR with targeted visual verification. Absence from this finite sample is not proof of a categorical ban.

## Current adaptive format

DOE’s current page specifies 50 ELA and 50 math items, 180 minutes, and adaptation beginning fall 2026. Students must answer before advancing. Math/standalone ELA items cannot be revisited; passage-set ELA can be edited within the set before submission. Subjects cannot be revisited once submitted. This is a format/content distinction: harder adaptive items do not authorize teaching an unstated higher-grade curriculum.

## Evidence limitations

- Current full-length practice forms are representative, not an exhaustive inventory of operational questions.
- No authoritative item-by-item prohibited-topic list was found.
- NYSED PDF downloads returned server errors during this check; cited NYSED passages were available in indexed primary-source excerpts. DOE/Pearson PDFs were downloaded and inspected directly.
- No copyrighted practice-test corpus is reproduced in this deliverable. Use original new questions when rewriting DrillMaster items.
